Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Aarya Solution ("we", "our", "us") handles information in connection with the Android applications we publish on Google Play (each, an "App"). This policy applies to all Apps published under this developer account unless a specific App states otherwise within the app itself.

Information we collect

Depending on the App, we may collect:

  • Information you provide — such as your name, email address, or content you enter directly into the App.
  • Automatically collected information — such as device type, operating system version, app usage, and crash or diagnostic logs, used to keep the App working correctly.
  • Permissions you grant — such as storage, camera, or location, only if the specific App requests them, and only used for the feature you enable.

We do not collect more than what a given App needs to function.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • To diagnose technical issues and fix bugs
  • To respond to support requests
  • To meet legal obligations

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us run the App (for example, app hosting, analytics, or crash-reporting services), and these providers are only permitted to use it to provide that service to us.

Data storage and security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your choices

You can withdraw permissions at any time through your device settings. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
